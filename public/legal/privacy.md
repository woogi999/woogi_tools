# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Woogi Tools is operated by one person as a personal project. It applies to the website at https://tools.woogi.xyz/ and the tools available there.

## Information processed by the tools

Woogi Tools is designed to work locally in your browser. Text, images, files, settings, favourites, notes, and other content you enter into a tool are processed on your device and are not sent to me or stored on a server by Woogi Tools. Some preferences and saved content may remain in your browser's local storage so the site can remember them between visits.

I do not use Woogi Tools to collect accounts, names, email addresses, analytics, advertising identifiers, browsing histories, or other personal information. I do not sell, rent, or share user information for advertising or profiling.

## Online games and peer connections

Some multiplayer features may connect browsers directly so that participants can play together. To help establish those connections, the service may use Cloudflare's TURN services. Cloudflare may receive your IP address and limited connection or relay metadata needed to provide the TURN service. TURN relays traffic so that your IP address is not exposed to other participants. Woogi Tools does not use that information to identify you, build a profile, or keep a database of it. Cloudflare handles this information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Apart from this limited networking needed for multiplayer features, the tools remain local to your device. You can use the non-multiplayer tools without sending their content to a server.

## Local storage and browser requests

The site may use browser storage for functionality such as favourites, notes, settings, and game preferences. You can remove this data through your browser settings or the site's reset controls. The browser may also request files needed to load the site and its optional libraries. Those requests are not used by me to create a personal profile.

## Children

Woogi Tools is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when the site or its services change. The date above shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the site after an update means you have seen the revised policy.
